S-Tier Visual Hook & Tourist Appeal

Look at the elements you placed in S-Tier. Why are these the most effective choices to make people want to discover and visit your country? How will they capture a viewer's eye from across the room?

Q2

Cultural Aspects Strategy (Curriculum Part B)

The project instructions require presenting at least 3 cultural aspects. Which 3 cultural elements (e.g. music/dance, famous person, traditional festival, clothing, monument, sports, fun fact) will you feature, and how will you write short English explanations rather than just a simple list?

Q3

English Country Poster Guidelines

The Challenge

Choose a country where English is one of the national or official languages and create an attractive, informative digital poster. Your poster should make people want to discover and visit your country!

1) Essential Information

  • Capital city: Clearly labeled, indicating the nation's political hub.
  • National flag: Authentic colors, proportions, and symbolic crosses or emblems.
  • National emblem / Coat of arms: Traditional crest or official motto.

2) Culture (Minimum 3 Aspects)

Present at least 3 cultural aspects. Important: Do not simply make a list. Write short explanations in English.

  • Music and dance traditions
  • Cinema and famous personalities
  • National sports and traditional games
  • Festivals, parades, and ancestral events
  • Traditional clothing and ceremonial textiles
  • Famous monuments, heritage landmarks, and architecture
  • Literature and surprising fun facts

3) Food & Gastronomy

  • 2 to 3 traditional savory or sweet dishes.
  • One typical regional drink or beverage.
  • Short explanations highlighting flavors, ingredients, and cultural origin.

4) Language & Style Rules

  • Short sentences with punchy, accessible phrasing.
  • Simple and precise vocabulary suited for an international travel poster.
  • Clear and visible titles with strong typographic hierarchy.
  • Correct spelling and grammar throughout.
  • Original wording: Do not copy and paste long texts from the internet; use your own words!
